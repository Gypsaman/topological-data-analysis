H(D)
Persistent Entropy
p_i = r_i / Σr_i
H(D) = −Σ p_i log(p_i)

Shannon entropy of normalized lifetimes. High entropy → complex, disordered attractor (volatile market). Low entropy → few dominant topological features (trend).

A(D)
Amplitude
A(D) = max_i r_i
 

The longest-lived topological feature. A large amplitude signals one dominant cycle or loop in the attractor — a strong, persistent market rhythm.

N(D)
Point Count
N(D) = |{i : r_i > 0}|
 

Number of finite persistence pairs. Measures the topological richness of the embedding — how many distinct structural features the market exhibits.

TDA Pipeline — Equations 1–3 p_i = r_i / Σ r_i    r_i = d_i − b_i    (finite lifetimes from H₀ ∪ H₁)
H(D) = −Σ p_i · log(p_i)    A(D) = max r_i    N(D) = |I_q|
v = [H(D), A(D), N(D)] ∈ ℝ³
